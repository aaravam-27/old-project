A Day I Almost Gave Up Coding

There was a point when I genuinely thought I was done with coding. Nothing was working the way I expected, every small bug seemed to create another one, and I had spent hours staring at the same screen.

I remember closing my laptop and walking away for a while. It wasn't really the code that bothered me. It was the feeling that I wasn't getting anywhere.

After a break, I came back and started going through the project from the beginning. Slowly, things started making sense. One mistake led to another discovery, and eventually the program worked again.

“I kept the number 4721 because it was the first project ID I ever used.”

Looking back, that day taught me something simple: getting stuck is part of learning. Sometimes the best thing you can do is step away, come back, and look at the problem from the beginning.

I didn't give up that day. I kept the project, kept the notes, and kept coding.
